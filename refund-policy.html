Refund Policy

Last updated:

30-day returns

If you're not happy with your candle, you can return any unused, unopened item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. You're responsible for return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

Damaged or incorrect orders

If your order arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, email us a photo within 7 days of delivery and we'll send a replacement or full refund, free of charge.

How to start a return

Email hello@candleist.com with your order number and the item(s) you'd like to return. We'll send return instructions within one business day.

Refund timing

Once we receive your return, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days back to your original payment method. It may take a few extra days for your bank to show the credit.

Order changes & cancellations

Need to change or cancel an order? Email us within 2 hours of purchase and we'll do our best to catch it before it ships.
