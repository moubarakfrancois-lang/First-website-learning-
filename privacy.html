Privacy Policy

Last updated:

What we collect

When you browse Candleist, we collect basic information like the pages you visit and items you add to your cart (stored locally in your browser). If you create an account, check out, or contact us, we collect the details you provide directly — such as your name, email, shipping address, and order history.

How we use it

We use your information to process and ship orders, respond to support requests, and improve the site. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Sharing

We share information only with the services required to run the store — for example, a payment processor to handle transactions and a shipping carrier to deliver your order. Each of those providers has its own privacy practices governing the data they process.

Cookies & local storage

Candleist uses your browser's local storage to remember your cart between visits. This data stays on your device and isn't sent to a server unless you complete checkout.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us at the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws like the GDPR or CCPA.
